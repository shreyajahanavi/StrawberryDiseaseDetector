Tips for Accurate Strawberry Foliage Photography

1
Optimal Lighting

Photograph in diffuse natural daylight. Avoid extreme harsh noon shadows or direct camera flash reflections.

2
Macro Focus

Fill at least 70% of the frame with the suspect strawberry leaflet, keeping necrotic lesions in sharp focus.

3
Both Lamina Surfaces

For suspected Powdery Mildew, capture the underside (abaxial) surface where white fungal mycelium originates.

4
Plain Background

Place a hand or plain cardboard behind the leaf if dense surrounding soil or mulch creates visual clutter.
