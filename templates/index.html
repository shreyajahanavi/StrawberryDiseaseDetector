How the System Works

A seamless four-tier architectural workflow designed for agricultural field conditions and rapid disease mitigation.

Step 01

Leaf Image Acquisition

Farmers upload a leaf photo or capture a live macro image directly in the field using their smartphone camera or tablet browser.

Drag-and-Drop & WebRTC Camera
Step 02

Enhanced ResNet9-SE

The leaf image is normalized to 224×224 and processed through residual blocks enhanced with Squeeze-and-Excitation attention to prioritize crucial pathogen channels.

Squeeze-and-Excitation Attention
Step 03

Grad-CAM Explainability

Gradients from the final residual convolutional layer generate a thermal activation heatmap, visually proving to farmers which leaf lesions dictated the diagnosis.

Explainable AI (XAI)
Step 04

Environmental Risk Fusion

Microclimate temperature and relative humidity telemetry (via ESP32 + DHT22) is evaluated against pathogen proliferation curves to provide actionable advisory.

Diseases the System Aims to Detect

Comprehensive identification of foliar threats affecting commercial strawberry (Fragaria × ananassa) production.

Target Class 0 Healthy Foliage

Healthy Strawberry Leaf

Fragaria × ananassa

Vigorous, unblemished trifoliate leaves with vibrant chlorophyll distribution, intact margins, and robust vascular structure free from lesions or discoloration.

Visual Indicators: Uniform deep emerald green, no necrosis, intact foliar cuticle.

Target Class 1 Fungal Pathogen

Leaf Scorch

Diplocarpon earlianum

Irregular purplish-brown lesions without white centers. In advanced stages, spots coalesce causing entire leaflets to curl upward and appear scorched or blighted.

Visual Indicators: Small purple flecks enlarging into dark necrotic patches across upper leaf lamina.
Preview Diagnosis Layout →
Target Class 2 Fungal Pathogen

Powdery Mildew

Podosphaera aphanis

Superficial white powdery fungal mycelium spreading across the underside of leaves, causing characteristic upward curling of leaflet edges and reddish-bronze foliar spots.

Visual Indicators: White powdery talc-like patches on abaxial surface, upward cupping of leaves.
Preview Diagnosis Layout →
Target Class 3 Bacterial Pathogen

Angular Leaf Spot

Xanthomonas fragariae

Major quarantine bacterial pathogen. Creates distinctive water-soaked lesions strictly delimited by veinlets. Translucent under backlighting with bacterial ooze when wet.

Visual Indicators: Geometric angular lesions bordered by veins; translucent when held against sunlight.
